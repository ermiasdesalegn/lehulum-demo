Try it as a learner

One lesson, four learners. Each button sets up the same player the way that learner would use it, using settings any learner can choose in the player. Press a button, then play the lesson.

Honest notes: the sign-language window shows a labelled placeholder clip, not sign language. These setups have not yet been tried by a blind, Deaf or low-vision learner. The setups are saved in this browser until you press "Back to normal".
