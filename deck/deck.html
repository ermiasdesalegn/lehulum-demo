An online lesson that shuts people out

We followed two learners from the game plan through one ordinary lesson. The untouched Savanna starter page shows the same gaps: a video with no captions, no signer, no description.

Hana, Adama

  • Deaf; Ethiopian Sign Language is her first language
  • No captions, no signer: she cannot follow

Dawit, Bahir Dar

  • Blind; uses TalkBack
  • Buttons have no names, so he cannot even start the video

Both

  • Nothing works offline
  • Heavy video on a slow phone
  • Nothing in Amharic

Hana and Dawit are personas from the game plan, not people we met.

What Track 2 asked for, and what is built

Keyboard and screen reader

  • Native controls, visible focus, 44 px targets

Captions

  • Amharic and English, resizable, recolourable

Sign language

  • Movable, resizable window kept in sync

Audio description

  • Video pauses; your own screen reader reads it

Offline and 3G

  • 8 KB lesson file, audio-only version, data saver

Handouts

  • Tagged PDF, Amharic Braille file

Career gateway

  • Job cards with a spoken summary

Certificate

  • Signed link anyone can verify, no personal data

The idea: one script, a person approves

1. One script

  • The instructor adds a lecture video once

2. Machines draft

  • Audio-only copy, speech to text, Amharic translation

3. A person approves

  • Every line has a named reviewer

4. Learners get it

  • Only approved text is ever shown

Our real 90-second test video gave 13 caption drafts and one mistake: the machine heard “A I” as “A E I”. That is why a person approves.

Try it now: live, no login

  • Lesson 1 player Press Play, then Captions. At 2:00 the video pauses and the description is read out.
  • Lesson 2 player A real 90-second video with Amharic and English captions and its sign-language window.
  • Instructor Studio Add a lecture and a sign video, watch them stay in sync, review the captions line by line.

Everything works with the keyboard alone. Nothing starts playing by itself.
