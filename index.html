Lehulum (ለሁሉም): public demo

Accessible learning media, ALX Digital Bridge, Track 2. Team: Ermias and Estifanos. No login required.

  • The lesson player on a plain page (captions in Amharic and English, descriptions, sign window, transcript; works with no server)
  • Amharic sample lesson: the player with a floating sign window, themes (dark, light, high contrast) and full screen
  • Recorded live-session viewer: captions and a searchable chat log; each chat message jumps the video to that moment
  • The slide deck

What is a sample. The sign-language picture in these demos is a labelled sample clip, not real sign language: we have no interpreter footage yet. The Amharic voice is synthetic, and the Amharic text is our draft, not yet reviewed by a native speaker. The 14 chat messages in the session viewer are invented. Everything here passed automated accessibility tests; nobody who is blind or Deaf has tested it yet.

The check question and the signed certificate need the API. They run from the submitted zip with Docker (docker compose up --build) and are shown live in the pitch.
